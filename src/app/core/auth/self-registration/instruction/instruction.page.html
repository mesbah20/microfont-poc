Sign Up Instructions

  1. Visit the nearest {{ displayName() }} branch with your valid photo ID (NID / Passport) and a recent passport-size photograph.
  2. Fill out the Account Opening Form provided at the branch.
  3. Submit your application — the bank will create your Customer ID.
  4. Default banking credentials (Customer ID + temporary password) will be sent via SMS to your registered mobile number.
  5. Visit the eBanking portal and log in with your credentials. You will be prompted to change the password on first login.
  6. For any assistance, call the 24/7 helpline: 16210.
